Settings

On: Grade 12 first, Kinder last. Off: Kinder first, Grade 12 last. Applies to every grade dropdown for everyone. Takes effect the next time a page is opened.
Asks teachers and students who have not sent feedback yet to rate MyGuro. It stops for each person once they send it. Never on the answer page.

School years

Classes and the work given to them belong to a school year. When you start a new year it becomes the current one: teachers and students begin with no classes, and past years can only be looked at (the year picker is in the account menu).

    Grades in use

    Turn off the grades your school doesn't have. A disabled grade is hidden from the dropdowns and can't be given to a student or a class. Students who already have it keep it, and the Students filter still lists it so you can move them.

    Support link

    A small "support" note at the bottom of each page for admin and teachers. Its button opens a window with the ways to give that you fill in below (Ko-fi, GCash). Students never see it.
    Opens the support window by itself. Only teachers see it, never students.

    Tips preview

    Teachers and students see one of these in a closable box under the menu, a new one every 3 days. They can close it or turn tips off. Never shown on the answer page.
